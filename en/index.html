FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions before you start?

The four questions I get asked most.

What do I need to provide to get started?

The essentials are your business content: logo (if you have one), texts, photos, contact details and social media. If you haven't written your texts yet, tell me what you do and I'll help you organize them. Also show me websites you like and the colors you prefer. We work with what exists and complete the rest as we go.

Will the site work well on a phone?

Yes. I always start from the phone (mobile-first) and then adapt to tablet and desktop. Most of your customers will open the site on their phone, so it's the first thing I optimize, in both looks and speed.

How much do hosting and the domain cost?

The domain (for example, yourbusinessname.com) is an annual cost paid to the domain registrar. Hosting for a simple native-code site can be very cheap or even free, for example on GitHub Pages. I take care of buying and setting it up, and these amounts are always made clear in the quote before we start.

How does payment work?

The price and terms are agreed in the quote before we start. I usually ask for a part up front to reserve your project, and the rest on delivery, after you have seen and approved the site. Monthly maintenance ($50 a month), if you want it, is paid separately.
